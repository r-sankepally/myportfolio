I have chosen Mizou.com as platform to build this AI chatbot.  Mindful AI chatbot helps user to identify stress and provides suggestions on mindfulness. 

It welcomes users with a text greeting message and interacts in a text-based chat.
 
Rules / parameters have been defined to keep the response short (under 100 words), ask questions on how they are feeling and their experiences to keep engaging and not to ask Personally identifiable information.
